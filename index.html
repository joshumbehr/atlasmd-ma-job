About the practice

AtlasMD is a physician-owned Direct Primary Care practice in Wichita. We do not bill insurance. Members pay a transparent monthly fee and get unhurried visits, same-day access, house calls when needed, wholesale labs and medications, and a doctor who actually knows them.

What we love most about this work is the relationship. Family medicine is meant to be delivered in a caring, unhurried setting. We partner with patients, listen first, and use shared decision-making so care fits real life — not a 7-minute slot and a billing code. If you want to practice the way you were taught care should look, this is that setting.

The role

You are the clinical right hand of the physician and often the first face a member sees. You keep the day flowing without making anyone feel rushed. You draw blood well. You notice when a patient is anxious and you make the room feel safe. You keep the clinic stocked, the labs moving, and the chart honest.

Clinical work

  • Room patients: history, medications, vitals, and a clear chief complaint in the EMR.
  • Perform phlebotomy independently — venipuncture and capillary sticks — with high first-stick success and a calm bedside manner. Required skill, not a nice-to-have.
  • Collect, label, process, and send lab specimens; run CLIA-waived in-office tests (UA, strep, flu, COVID, glucose, pregnancy, and similar).
  • Prepare send-outs, complete requisitions, and coordinate courier pickup.
  • Give immunizations and injections (IM, SQ) as ordered.
  • EKG, peak flow/spirometry, pulse ox, vision screening, wound care, suture/staple removal, cryotherapy and laceration tray setup, and other primary-care procedures.
  • Assist during exams; anticipate what is needed next.
  • Document the same day. Flag abnormal results and messages promptly.
  • Educate members in plain language — labs, vaccines, home instructions, how membership works.

Clinic operations

  • Keep rooms clean, stocked, and ready. Track inventory and expiration dates.
  • Manage lab logs, quality control, and specimen tracking.
  • Help with scheduling, check-in/out, phone and portal messages, and referrals — without insurance prior-auth theater.
  • Protect privacy. Follow HIPAA as if the chart were your own family's.
  • Support house-call or off-site visit prep when needed.
  • Other duties that keep a small, high-trust practice running well.

What success looks like

  • Patients leave saying they felt heard — and their blood draw did not hurt more than it had to.
  • The physician can stay in the room with the patient because you already handled the rest.
  • Labs go out correctly labeled the first time. Results do not sit.
  • The clinic looks ready at 8:00 a.m. and still looks ready at 4:55 p.m.
  • You treat members like neighbors, not throughput.

Required

  • High school diploma or GED.
  • Documented competence in phlebotomy. You must draw blood independently on day one. A skills check is part of hiring.
  • Current BLS/CPR, or obtained within 30 days of hire.
  • Comfort with an EMR and ordinary clinic software.
  • Reliable transportation and ability to work on-site in Wichita.
  • Legal authorization to work in the United States.

Preferred

  • CMA, RMA, NCMA, CCMA, or equivalent certification.
  • 1+ year in primary care, family medicine, urgent care, or another outpatient clinic.
  • Experience across the lifespan, including pediatrics and older adults.
  • Kansas-appropriate medication and vaccine administration experience.
  • Interest in Direct Primary Care and relationship-based medicine — not a high-volume insurance mill.

Schedule, pay, setting

Full-time, weekday clinic hours (typical 8:00–5:00 with a real lunch). Occasional flexibility for late patients or house-call support. Small team, Wichita locations. No RVU treadmill. No “see 30 before noon.”

Competitive hourly pay based on experience and phlebotomy skill. Paid time off. Employee membership discount for AtlasMD care. Details with qualified candidates.
